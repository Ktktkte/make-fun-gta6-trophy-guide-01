Roadmap to the platinum — about 200 hours, one playthrough

No difficulty settings, no New Game+, no mission replay — and no trophy depends on how you play a mission. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep them off. Free roam carries on after the credits, and the 100% checklist only appears under Progress > Stats after the story. (Sources: PowerPyx — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap, GTABase — GTA 6 Trophy Guide.)

  1. Stage 1 — Play the story, and take side content as it shows up 60–80 hours

    Play the five chapters in order. The 12 story trophies land here: the opening mission Gone Fishing, the four chapter trophies, the final mission, and the six named missions (Breakfast Run in Chapter 2; The Cartel Heist in Chapter 3; Child's Play, Headliners and VCIA Takedown in Chapter 4; Operation Savage Glory in Chapter 5). Do the Chapter 1 meditation for Positive Mental Attitude — the one missable trophy. Start the long counts early: scan vehicles and WAiNK spots with the WAiNK app, meditate at every spot, and take each character questline (Cal, Kenton, Andy, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret) the moment the character contacts you — several are only offered for a while.

    Stage 1 trophies: the 12 story trophies, plus Positive Mental Attitude, Master of None and Stick Fiddler as you go.

  2. Stage 2 — Finish the 100% checklist about 100 hours

    After the credits, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion and work through what is left. The checklist is lenient — closer to 60% true completion. It demands in full: every robbery (all 8 bank vaults and all 39 gang operations), all underwater discoveries, every Shooting Range gold medal, all drone races and all 15 of Bret's Target Decks. Everything else is partial: 100 optional missions, 200 of 277 vehicle scans, 100 of 255 animal studies, 15 of 18 meditations, 70 of 80 challenges, 300 stunts and 100 WAiNK scans. Most of the miscellaneous trophies pop along the way.

    Stage 2 trophies: A Long Road (gold) and the bulk of the activity, collectible, combat and money trophies.

  3. Stage 3 — Strangers & Freaks and clean-up about 10 hours

    Stranger missions are not part of 100%, but Strangers & Freaks needs 8 qualifying strands (Kirk, Kyle, Yago and Pilot Pete count; the character questlines do not). Finish the character questlines — Andy (Lucia-only, Chapter 3), Kenton (7 shipwrecks), Billy (5 jobs), Randy (16 sports cars), Tyrone (synthetic labs), Bret (Target Decks) and Cal (fishing challenge) — then check the list below for anything left. The platinum pops with the last trophy.

    Stage 3 trophies: the 8 questline trophies and Strangers & Freaks (silver).

    About this guide

    Official names, descriptions and grades are shown exactly as PlayStation publishes them — the list as carried by the trophy trackers Exophase and PSNProfiles and reproduced in the All.gg full PSN list. Every guide is written in our own words for this entry; nothing is copied from another guide. Where a guide's facts come from a published guide, the source is named and linked beside the trophy — mainly the GTABase GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Achievements List and the PowerPyx GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap. Where those guides say the exact steps are not yet confirmed, this guide says so instead of inventing a location or a step.

    There are no online trophies: every trophy is earned in single-player. 28 of the 55 are hidden on PlayStation until earned — this guide marks them, and the spoiler setting hides their names and descriptions until you check them off.

    This guide is a static offline app: it loads only its own files, fetches nothing from the network, and saves your checklist in your browser (localStorage). Export downloads a JSON file of your progress; import reads that file back. No account, no tracking, no Rockstar artwork — the look is our own.
